Our work

Systems We Have Built for Operations

Corelane Systems has delivered HRM CMS platforms, education portals, business websites, inventory management systems, and social media programs. This page describes those project types. It does not name clients, show live product screenshots, or invent performance statistics.

Let’s Talk View services

How to read this page

The work below is real in kind: these are systems and programs we have designed, built, or operated. What we will not do here is invent a logo wall, a named case study, or a percentage improvement. If a client later agrees to a public write-up, it will be labelled as a verified case study.

Photographs are workplace atmosphere. They are not screenshots of a live HRM, portal, store, or social dashboard.

Human resources

HRM CMS

We have built human-resource management systems that hold employee records in a permissioned CMS rather than in scattered files. Typical scope includes roles and access, employee profiles, attendance and leave, and the workflows HR teams actually run day to day.

The aim is a system of record HR can operate: who is on the books, who approved what, and what happens when a request moves between manager and administrator. We do not publish headcount, payroll volumes, or named employers here.

Related service: custom software development

Education

Education portals

We have delivered education portals for programme, learner, and administration work. Typical scope includes enrolment and records, programme information, staff and learner access, and the internal tools administrators use when a public brochure site is not enough.

Portals of this kind sit on authenticated access and a clear system of record. We do not list campuses, student numbers, or named institutions on this page.

Related industry: education · Web applications

Web presence

Business websites

We have designed and developed business websites that explain an offer clearly, stay maintainable after launch, and give editors a content structure they can actually use. Typical scope includes information architecture, accessible front end, and a public site that can later grow into an application if logins are required.

A website is not a substitute for an operations system. When the work needs roles, inventory, or HR data, that is a different engagement — and we say so. We do not claim search rankings or traffic numbers we have not measured with you.

Related service: website development

Operations

Inventory management

We have built inventory management systems for stock, orders, and the hand-offs between purchasing, warehouse, and sales. Typical scope includes item records, stock visibility, receiving and fulfilment steps, and connections to the tools a business already uses for orders or accounting.

The constraint is usually the same: spreadsheets and inboxes cannot keep a reliable picture of what is on hand. We do not invent warehouse throughputs, SKU counts, or named retailers here.

Related service: custom software · Business automation

Brand and channels

Social media management

We have planned and operated social media programs that support a brand with a publishing cadence, not a burst of unrelated posts. Typical scope includes channel mix, content direction aligned to the website, a schedule the team can keep, and simple reporting on activity.

Social work is useful when it points back to a clear offer. It is not a substitute for software, inventory, or HR systems. We do not publish invented reach, follower, or engagement figures on this site.

Related service: social media management
