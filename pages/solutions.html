FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I don’t know what technology solution I need?

That’s completely fine. You can start by explaining the business problem, current process, or desired outcome. We can help identify the appropriate technology approach.

Can you work with our existing software?

Yes. Depending on the system, we can integrate, improve, extend, modernize, or migrate existing technology.

Can you automate manual business processes?

Yes. We can analyze repetitive workflows and identify opportunities for automation, integration, centralization, and better process management.

Can you connect different business systems?

Yes. We can use APIs, integrations, webhooks, and data workflows to connect compatible systems.

Can you build software specifically for our business?

Yes. Custom software can be designed around your workflows, users, data, permissions, reporting, and operational requirements.

Do you provide Cloud and DevOps services?

Yes. Our Cloud and DevOps capabilities include infrastructure, deployment automation, CI/CD, containers, monitoring, and operational improvements.

How do we get started?

Tell us about the problem you’re trying to solve, the systems you currently use, and the outcome you want to achieve. We can then discuss the requirements and possible next steps.
